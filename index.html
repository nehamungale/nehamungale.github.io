Where in the loop should an LLM sit?

My MSc thesis at UCL School of Pharmacy, with Dr. David Shorthouse. Antibody formulation campaigns burn 40 to 100 irreversible experiments, so I tested six places an LLM's knowledge could enter a Bayesian optimisation loop. Click a point to see what happened.

Call the LLM once, before the first experiment. After that, let the data lead.

Closing the loop in hardware

The next step is a physical loop: an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handler prepares formulations and an xArm5 robot arm, guided by a depth camera on ROS2, carries plates to instruments such as a SpectraMax plate reader. I built the ArUco marker registration and motion planning for the arm to navigate across the lab.

Motion planning for the xArm5, validated in a digital twin of the lab setup before running on the real arm.
